Responsibilities

  • Collaborated with business stakeholders for requirement validation, tracked and documented changes to specifications, supported SIT/UAT sign-off, and maintained requirement traceability matrices ahead of each release.
  • Followed up on system incidents and coordinated with development teams for timely fixes — treating production issues as top priority, replicating them in the test environment with proper data, and releasing back only once testing confirmed no remaining defects.
